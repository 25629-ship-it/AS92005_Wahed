Mt Roskill Guardianship

Our Mission

Pest B Control shares information to help people in Mt Roskill manage pests responsibly and look after the local environment.

Kaitiakitanga

Kaitiakitanga means guardianship and caring for the environment. We encourage people to protect native plants, birds and other wildlife when dealing with pests.

Manaakitanga

Manaakitanga means showing care and respect for people. We share clear information so families and neighbours can help each other keep homes and shared spaces clean and safe.

Small Actions Make a Difference

  • Keep food in sealed containers.
  • Put rubbish in closed bins.
  • Ask for advice before using pest treatments.
  • Choose methods carefully to avoid harming native wildlife.
