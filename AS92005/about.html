About Pest B Control

This website is for people in Mt Roskill who want to learn about common pests and ways to manage them responsibly.

Kaitiakitanga and Manaakitanga

Kaitiakitanga

Kaitiakitanga is guardianship and care for the environment. On this website, it means encouraging pest control choices that protect native plants, birds and other wildlife.

Manaakitanga

Manaakitanga is showing care, kindness and respect to people. This website supports it by giving clear information that families and neighbours can use to help each other.

Working Together

People can help their community by keeping areas clean, sharing useful advice and thinking about the effects of pest control on other living things.
